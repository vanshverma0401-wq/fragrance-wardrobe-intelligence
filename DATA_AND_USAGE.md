# Data and usage

## Included demonstration data

`data/demo_collection.csv` and `data/demo_wear_log.csv` are original synthetic
demonstration data created for this project. All houses, fragrances, prices,
ratings, attributes, and events are fictional. The files are included to make
validation, tests, and commands reproducible without distributing a real
person's collection or relying on a third-party commercial database.

The demo contains eight fictional collection items and eighteen fictional wear
events. It is not evidence of consumer behavior or fragrance-market results.

## User-owned data

The empty templates can be copied and filled with data the user owns or has the
right to process:

- [collection template](data/collection_template.csv)
- [wear-log template](data/wear_log_template.csv)

Before publishing a filled version, remove purchase details, locations, account
identifiers, notes about other people, or any other personal information that is
not necessary for the project.

## External enrichment

No external dataset is included in the MVP. A future revision may add optional,
reproducible enrichment from a source with clear reuse terms. Any such addition
must record the source, fields, retrieval date, transformation, and license or
reuse statement before its outputs are published.

## Usage boundary

The software and fictional demo data are provided under the repository license.
The project does not grant rights to third-party fragrance databases, brand
assets, product photography, or descriptions that may be introduced by a user.
